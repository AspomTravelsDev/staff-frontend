# Staff Portal

## Compliance

**Incident**: A documented event submitted for review. An incident can require correction without qualifying for disciplinary occurrences.

**Occurrence**: A verified breach counted toward the disciplinary ladder. Its approved value may be fractional.

**Correction plan**: The work required to address an incident, with a deadline and completion verification independent of its disciplinary outcome.

**Repeated mistake**: An error similar to one for which correction or coaching was previously documented.



**Recommended step**: The disciplinary step indicated by the employee's occurrence total, subject to a recorded human decision.

**Recorded sanction**: A disciplinary action actually confirmed by an authorized reviewer, distinct from a recommendation.

**Re-entered threshold**: An occurrence threshold reached again after a previous action and a subsequent reduction in the rolling total. It requires review and does not itself justify greater punishment.

**Payroll deduction**: An approved reduction expressed in days of pay. Every eight accumulated confirmed occurrences converts to one deduction day. A suspension contributes its working days directly and adds no occurrence points. Unused occurrence points carry across payroll months, separately from the rolling disciplinary window.
